How to Pay Your Progressive Bill Online

These step-by-step instructions are written for people who want a clear, no-nonsense process.

Steps (account holders)

  1. Open Progressive's official site (type progressive.com yourself) and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the "Billing & Payments" or "Pay My Bill" area to confirm the amount due and the due date.
  3. Choose a payment method: bank/ACH, debit, or credit. Bank/ACH is commonly the cheapest option.
  4. Complete payment and save the confirmation number; take a screenshot and keep the receipt email.

Quick tips

  • Prefer ACH/bank transfer when you want to avoid card convenience fees.
  • Do not use payment links from unknown texts or emails — navigate to progressive.com directly.

Pay as a Guest (one-time payment)

If you cannot sign in, you can usually pay with your policy number and billing ZIP code.

  1. Locate your policy number on a mailed or emailed statement.
  2. At Progressive's official payment page choose the "Guest Payment" or similar option.
  3. Enter your policy number, billing ZIP, payment details, and submit. Save the confirmation number.

Pay by Phone

Pay by phone if you prefer to confirm details with a representative. Have your policy number and billing ZIP ready.

How to pay by phone

  1. Find the official billing phone number on Progressive's website or your billing statement.
  2. Call and follow prompts to pay by card or bank account, or request to speak with an agent for assistance.
  3. Ask for and save the confirmation number and the agent's name if applicable.

Login Help — regain access securely

If you cannot sign in, these straightforward steps can help you get back into your account.

  1. On progressive.com click "Forgot Username or Password."
  2. Follow the verification flow (email, phone, or policy number) and reset your password.
  3. Enable multi-factor authentication if available and use a strong, unique password.

Troubleshooting — if a payment fails or you suspect a scam

If a payment fails

  1. Confirm card expiration, billing ZIP, and CVV are correct.
  2. Try another payment method or contact Progressive through official channels if the issue persists.
  3. Keep proof of attempted payment (screenshots, bank statement) in case you need to dispute.

If you suspect a scam

  • Do not follow links or call numbers that arrived in suspicious emails or texts; go to progressive.com directly or use the number on your bill.
  • Progressive will not demand payment by gift cards or cryptocurrency for standard premiums.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay my Progressive bill without logging in?
Yes. Use the guest payment option with your policy number and billing ZIP. Save the confirmation number and the email receipt.
What is Progressive's bill pay phone number?
For billing and payment help call 1-888-414-4406. Always verify any number you call against progressive.com or the phone number printed on your billing statement.
Is there a fee to pay my Progressive bill online?
Fees vary by payment method. Bank/ACH is often free; card payments may have convenience fees. Confirm fees on the payment screen before submitting.
I can't log in. How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Username or Password" on progressive.com and follow the verification steps. If you still can't access your account, contact Progressive using the official contact methods on their site.
How do I confirm my payment went through?
Save the confirmation number, take a screenshot of the confirmation page, and check your email for a receipt from Progressive.
