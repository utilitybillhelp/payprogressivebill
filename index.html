Pay Progressive Online — step-by-step

If you have access to your account, follow these simple steps. Bullet answers make it easy to scan.

Quick steps

  • Go to Progressive's official site: https://www.progressive.com/ (type this into your browser).
  • Sign in and open "Billing & Payments".
  • Choose payment method (bank/ACH, debit, credit). For lowest cost pick ACH when available.
  • Confirm payment, save confirmation number and email receipt.

Short answers

  • How fast? Most online payments process immediately or within one business day.
  • Fees? ACH/bank is often free; card payments can have convenience fees — check before confirming.
  • Safety? Always confirm the site is progressive.com and check for HTTPS in the address bar.

Helpful external sources: Progressive billing, Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.

Pay as a Guest (one-time payment)

Guest payments use your policy number and billing ZIP — no account sign-in required.

Steps

  • Find your policy number on the bill or statement.
  • Go to Progressive's payment page and choose "Guest Payment" or equivalent.
  • Enter policy number, billing ZIP, payment method, submit, and save confirmation.

Short answers

  • When use it? If you can't sign in and need to make a one-time payment quickly.
  • What to keep? Confirmation number and screenshot of the confirmation page.

Pay by Phone — speak with a billing specialist

If you prefer to confirm details with a person or need help resolving an issue, call Progressive billing support.

How to pay by phone

  • Call Progressive's billing number from your bill or the official site.
  • Have your policy number, billing ZIP, and payment method ready.
  • Ask for a confirmation number and the agent's name; keep both for your records.

Progressive billing phone: 1-888-414-4406

Short answers

  • Why call? Complex issues, disputes, or when online payments fail.
  • Expected wait? Varies by time of day — have your info ready to speed the call.

Verify this number against progressive.com or your billing statement before calling.

Login Help — regain secure access

Quick steps

  • Use "Forgot Username or Password" on progressive.com and follow verification prompts.
  • Try alternate emails or phone numbers you may have used.
  • If account locked, contact Progressive support (see phone above) to verify identity and restore access.

Troubleshooting — failed payments & suspected scams

If a payment fails

  • Confirm card details (expiration, CVV, billing ZIP).
  • Try another payment or use the pay-by-phone option 1-888-414-4406 for immediate processing.
  • Keep proof of attempted payment for dispute purposes (screenshots, bank records).

If you suspect a scam

  • Do not click links in suspicious messages. Go directly to progressive.com or call the number on your bill.
  • Progressive will not demand payment via gift cards or cryptocurrency for standard premiums.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay my Progressive bill without logging in?
Yes — use the guest payment form with your policy number and billing ZIP. Save the confirmation number and your receipt email.
What is Progressive's bill pay phone number?
Progressive billing support: 1-888-414-4406. Always verify any number with progressive.com or your billing statement before calling.
Is paying online free?
Bank/ACH payments are often free. Card payments may have convenience fees — the payment page will show any fees before you confirm.
How do I confirm a payment?
Save the confirmation number, screenshot the confirmation page, and check your email for Progressive's receipt.
